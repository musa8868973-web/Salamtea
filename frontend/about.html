About Us

♥
♥

Our story begins in the streets of Peshawar in 1955, when Haji Abdul Salam laid the foundation of Salamtea. With a deep passion for quality, he imported fine tea directly from Kenya, China, and Vietnam, bringing pure, authentic flavors to homes across Pakistan.

♥

His dedication earned the trust of an entire community. Customers travelled from far and wide, and the daily crowds outside his shop made it a local legend. That trust was earned cup by cup, year after year.

♥

Today, that legacy is carried forward by Rizwan Shahid and family with the same integrity and dedication.

♥

Now, as the next generation steps in, Salamtea is entering an exciting new chapter. While proudly continuing our wholesale business, we are now bringing our fine tea directly to your doorstep.

♥

Salamtea — A Matter of Good Taste, since 1955.
